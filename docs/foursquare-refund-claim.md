- Self-serve refund of unused **paid** credits is only documented **on account
  cancellation, within 30 days**. A duplicate-charge refund is a support-ticket case.
- Sources: [Developer Master Terms](https://foursquare.com/legal/terms/enterprise-developermasterterms/) ·
  [Manage Organization Billing](https://docs.foursquare.com/developer/docs/manage-organization-billing)
